GEOGRAPHIC DATA
Louisville/Jefferson County building rooflines and heights: LOJIC Open Data,
Spring 2024 imagery (published 2025).
https://gis.lojic.org/maps/rest/services/LojicSolutions/OpenDataBase/MapServer/8
Indiana-side building footprints: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Downloaded September 7, 2026. Louisville map bounds: -85.856,38.154,-85.661,38.352.
Bridge geometry is a lightweight game approximation. Ohio River and stream
shorelines use the official LOJIC water polygons cited above.

Bare-earth elevation for both maps: Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed through the
Registry of Open Data on AWS on September 7, 2026.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Terrain data attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
Elevation is sampled into compact local grids and vertically emphasized 1.15x.

Building footprints, NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI).
Source: https://services6.arcgis.com/yG5s3afENB5iO9fj/arcgis/rest/services/BUILDING_view/FeatureServer
Metadata: https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md
Downloaded September 6, 2026. Bounding box: -74.025,40.698,-73.955,40.785.
32,629 features. Roof heights are converted from feet to meters.
NYC Open Data terms: https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/#termsofuse

Shoreline/borough boundaries: NYC Department of City Planning, distributed via
https://github.com/dwillis/nyc-maps/blob/master/boroughs.geojson
Data is a historical snapshot, not a current navigational map.
Geographic data remains subject to its source terms; it is not relicensed CC0.
Facades and bridge details are illustrative. Detailed buildings cover Lower and
Midtown Manhattan and adjacent waterfronts, not all five boroughs.
